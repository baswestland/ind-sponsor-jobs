English-speaking jobs at IND recognised sponsors

Open vacancies written in English at employers that are on the official IND public register of recognised sponsors (work). These employers are allowed to sponsor a residence permit for highly skilled migrants and other work-related permits.

Sponsor list updated: 3 November 2026 · Vacancies updated: 2026-11-26

Being a recognised sponsor means the employer can sponsor a work permit; it does not guarantee that every vacancy is open to international candidates. Always check the vacancy text and the official register at ind.nl.

Vacancies are collected weekly from public company career pages and from Adzuna. Job function, field, industry and region are assigned automatically and may contain mistakes.
